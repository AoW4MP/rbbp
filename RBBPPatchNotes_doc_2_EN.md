## [martialdiscipline] (New) Martial Discipline

- Optional realm trait
- In battles involving human players, each side can field up to 5 units of each type and 4 heroes. At the end of the first round, randomly selected excess units retreat. These limits are shared by all players on the same side and do not apply to neutral armies or wonder defenders.
